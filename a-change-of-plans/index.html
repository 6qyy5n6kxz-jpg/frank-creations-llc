Who Is A Change Of Plans?

A Change Of Plans is a Northwest Ohio live-music act featuring Devin Frank and Kendra German. The act can perform as a solo or duo and features vocals, guitar, and piano for venues, weddings, private events, churches, festivals, and community gatherings.

For Devin Frank's dedicated solo-performance schedule and information, visit Devin Frank Live ↗.

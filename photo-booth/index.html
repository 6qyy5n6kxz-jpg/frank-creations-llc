Photo Booth FAQ

Common questions before booking.

Still unsure what fits? Send the date and event details and we can narrow it down after checking availability.

How much does a photo booth rental cost?

Photo booth rentals start at $350 for two hours, $450 for three hours, and $525 for four hours. Additional time starts at $75 per hour.

What is included?

Standard rentals include delivery and setup within the standard service area, unlimited digital captures during the rental window, a custom event overlay, instant text or email sharing when connectivity allows, a digital event gallery, teardown, and pickup.

Does the booth print physical photos?

No. The current Frank Creations booth is a digital experience. Guests can receive captures by text or email when connectivity allows, and the host receives a digital gallery.

Is an attendant required?

No. The standard experience is self-guided after Frank Creations delivers, sets up, and tests the booth. Attendant service may be available by request for larger or more complex events.

Can it be used outdoors?

Outdoor use may be possible when the booth has proper shelter, safe weather conditions, standard electrical power, and suitable connectivity for sharing.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

The booth can capture photos without guest Wi-Fi, but instant text and email delivery depends on usable internet or cellular connectivity at the event.

Photo Booth FAQ

Questions people usually ask before booking.

Still unsure whether the booth fits your event? Send the date and what you are planning. Availability can be checked before you make any booking decision.

How much does a photo booth rental cost?

Frank Creations photo booth rentals start at $350 for two hours, $450 for three hours, and $525 for four hours. Additional time starts at $75 per hour.

What is included?

Standard rentals include delivery and setup within the standard service area, unlimited digital captures during the rental window, a custom event overlay, instant text or email sharing when connectivity allows, a digital event gallery, teardown, and pickup.

Does the booth print physical photos?

The current Frank Creations booth is a digital experience. Guests can receive captures by text or email when connectivity allows, and the host receives a digital event gallery.

Can I customize the photo design?

Yes. Standard rentals include a custom overlay that can incorporate names, dates, school colors, wedding details, themes, or organization branding.

Is an attendant required?

No. The standard experience is designed to be self-guided after Frank Creations delivers, sets up, and tests the booth. Attendant service may be available by request for larger or more complex events.

Can the booth be used outdoors?

Outdoor use may be possible when there is a stable covered setup area, safe weather conditions, standard electrical power, and suitable connectivity for instant sharing.

Does the booth need Wi-Fi?

The booth can capture photos without guest Wi-Fi, but instant text and email delivery depends on a usable internet or cellular connection at the event.

How much space should I plan for?

Space needs depend on the venue and the group sizes you expect. Share the layout during your inquiry and Frank Creations can confirm an appropriate placement area.

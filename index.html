What Is Frank Creations?

Frank Creations LLC is a locally owned Northwest Ohio event company offering photo booth rentals, event enhancements, live music, Wine & Canvas experiences, Cookies & Canvas events, and coordinated packages for private and public gatherings.

What Areas Does Frank Creations Serve?

Frank Creations serves Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan, including Toledo, Genoa, Oak Harbor, Port Clinton, Perrysburg, Sylvania, Bowling Green, Oregon, Maumee, Monroe, and surrounding communities.

About Frank Creations

Bringing People Together Across Northwest Ohio

Frank Creations LLC is a locally owned event company serving Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan. The company brings together live music, creative experiences, photo booth rentals, event enhancements, and private-event support through one connected local brand.

What Is Frank Creations LLC?

Frank Creations LLC is a Northwest Ohio event company that helps individuals, businesses, schools, churches, and community organizations create memorable gatherings. Services include photo booth rentals, event enhancements, live music, Wine & Canvas experiences, Cookies & Canvas events, and coordinated event packages.

Our Goal

Frank Creations exists to make local events easier to plan and more enjoyable to attend. Whether the event is a graduation party, wedding, private celebration, community gathering, church event, or corporate function, the goal is to bring several useful services together through one local point of contact.

Where We Work

Frank Creations serves communities throughout Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan, including Toledo, Genoa, Oak Harbor, Port Clinton, Perrysburg, Sylvania, Bowling Green, Oregon, Maumee, Monroe, and surrounding areas.

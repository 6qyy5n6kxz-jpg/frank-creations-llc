About Frank Creations

Bringing People Together Across Northwest Ohio

Frank Creations LLC brings event services, entertainment, and creative experiences together under one local company—making celebrations across Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan easier to plan.

Built around making local events easier.

Frank Creations grew from several event and entertainment services that naturally began working together. Today, those offerings can stand on their own or be combined into one coordinated event plan through a single local point of contact.

That means fewer vendors to track down, fewer disconnected conversations, and more flexibility when an event needs entertainment, creative experiences, rentals, or practical support.

Book one service, choose a featured package, or build a custom combination around your event.

Local ownership. One growing idea.

Frank Creations LLC is owned by Devin Frank, whose work across live music, creative events, and local celebrations eventually grew into one connected event company.

Through Wine & Canvas Toledo, Devin Frank Live, A Change Of Plans, and Frank Creations event services, the goal is simple: make it easier for people to bring memorable local events together without coordinating more vendors than necessary.

Devin remains directly involved in the planning, development, and delivery of the company's services.

Serving Northwest Ohio & Southeast Michigan

Frank Creations serves communities throughout Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan, including Toledo, Genoa, Oak Harbor, Port Clinton, Perrysburg, Sylvania, Bowling Green, Oregon, Maumee, Monroe, and surrounding areas.

Travel outside the standard service area may be available depending on the event and services requested.
